Approvals

Prototype: employees decide what to do from needs, personality and time of day. Chats, task reports and HR pitches are written live by Claude once you allow it; without permission, simple fallback replies are used. Daily life runs on its own, but task requests, raise requests and job applications arrive as forms that need your signature, and HR screens applications first. Your world saves to your account automatically. When you come back after time away, the page catches up on everything that would have happened (up to 3 days), at 1 game day per 1h 30m.
